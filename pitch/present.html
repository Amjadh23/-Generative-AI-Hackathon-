RouteIQ

AI sales visit copilot for Hilti field teams. The most valuable route — not just the shortest.

  • Phone-first PWA: Today, Map, Customer
  • Voice → structured recap + Ask RouteIQ
  • Manager view: CRM signal vs real visits

The problem

Reps optimize drive time. Managers need pipeline truth. CRM scores miss what just happened on site.

  • Planning tools don’t rank visit value for the day.
  • Outcomes stay in people’s heads — not structured for the team.
  • Static scores don’t reflect satisfied visits and next steps.

What RouteIQ does

  • Today + map — prioritized stops, expected return, one-tap navigate.
  • AI in the field — speak a note, get a structured recap; ask grounded questions.
  • Manager story — “before CRM-only” vs “after visits & recaps” on future potential.

Rep workflow

Need RouteIQ
Where to go Value-ranked plan + map
Why this stop Expected return + impact framing
Fast logging Voice note → AI recap
On-site questions Ask RIQ on your data

Intelligence layer

  • RouteIQ score — heuristic blend of pipeline, behavior, and fit (demo-ready).
  • Value-aware routing — practical stop order, not just shortest path.
  • Line: Google Maps gets you there; RouteIQ tells you why this stop matters.

Voice recap & Ask RIQ

  • Rep records a short note after the visit.
  • LLM (e.g. Ollama) extracts structured fields — less typing, more truth in CRM.
  • Assistant answers from app context — fewer generic hallucinations.

Manager dashboard

Simple hackathon narrative: future potential looks low on CRM-only baseline; it rises when visits and recaps tell a satisfied, active story.

  • Traceable weights: outcomes + recap rows + structured next steps.
  • Mobile-friendly — same shell as the rep app.

Stack

  • React + TypeScript PWA
  • FastAPI + SQLite (KL-style synthetic dataset, ~400 accounts)
  • MapLibre + routing for visual proof
  • Local LLM for recap + chat in the demo

Why Hilti

  • Premium relationships — scale quality of every visit.
  • Managers see lift from field behavior in one glance.
  • Architecture ready for real data sources later.
